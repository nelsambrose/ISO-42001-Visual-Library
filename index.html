What's New in the ISO 42001 Visual Library

May 2026: Twelve new AI Principles cards covering the six core responsible AI concepts — Fairness, Transparency, Accountability, Human Oversight, Privacy, and Safety and Reliability — each available as a professional infographic and a humorous version. These complement the ISO/IEC 42001 clause and Annex A material with the underlying AI governance principles auditors and practitioners refer to most often.

Also recent: the Audit Readiness mini-deck — five card pairs on what an ISO 42001 auditor actually looks for, evidence versus good intentions, strong versus weak evidence examples, common audit failure modes across Annex A, and how to talk about controls confidently during a certification audit.

Coming next: reference cards on the ISO 42001 certification path, ISO 42001 versus ISO 27001, EU AI Act alignment, common implementation failure modes, and AI policy templates. See the full changelog on GitHub.

ISO 42001 Annex A Domain Cards

Annex A is the operational core of ISO 42001. Controls are grouped into four domains: Governance, Organisation, Operation, and Relationships. Organisations select controls based on their AI risks, context, and objectives.
